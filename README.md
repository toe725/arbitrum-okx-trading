## What Arbitrum (ARB) actually is

Arbitrum is a Layer 2 scaling solution for Ethereum, built on optimistic rollups. Transactions are processed off-chain and posted back to Ethereum in batches, which is why fees on Arbitrum are a fraction of what you'd pay on Ethereum mainnet while still inheriting Ethereum's security. The network can theoretically handle up to around 4,800 transactions per second.

The ARB token is the governance token of the Arbitrum ecosystem. Total supply is 10 billion, with roughly 6.68 billion in circulation as of mid-2026. For most of its history ARB has been a governance-only token — holders vote on proposals in the Arbitrum DAO. That's slowly changing: the community has been moving toward implementing ARB staking to give the token more on-chain utility, though that's still in progress and not fully live.

One thing worth knowing before you buy: ARB has a multi-year vesting and unlock schedule. The next major unlock is set for around September 2026, releasing roughly 1.2–1.4% of total supply. Large unlocks have historically put downward pressure on price, so if you're timing an entry, the unlock calendar is worth a glance.

## Things to think about before you buy ARB

**Token utility is still mostly governance.** If you're buying ARB because you expect it to capture Arbitrum network fees or revenue, that's not how the token works today. ARB is a governance token. Staking utility is being discussed and voted on by the Arbitrum DAO, but it's not live in a meaningful way as of mid-2026. Price action has largely been driven by L2 sector sentiment, Ethereum L2 TVL flows, and token unlock dynamics rather than by ARB-specific cash flows.

**Unlock pressure is real.** The vesting schedule runs into 2027. Each major unlock releases over 1% of total supply into the market. Historically, ARB has tended to soften around unlock dates. If you're buying a large amount, it's worth checking the next unlock date on a tokenomics tracker before committing.

**Arbitrum's ecosystem is genuinely active.** DefiLlama puts Arbitrum's DeFi TVL in the $1.5–2 billion range through mid-2026, with protocols like GMX and Vertex driving a lot of the derivatives volume. That's real usage — not just speculation — and it's part of why ARB has stayed one of the more liquid L2 tokens even after the steep drawdown from its all-time high of $2.405 (set in January 2024). As of this writing ARB trades around $0.11, roughly 95% below that high.

**Price predictions are not promises.** You'll find ARB price forecasts ranging from $0.06 to $0.12 for late 2026, and wider ranges beyond. These are model outputs, not commitments. Treat them as one input among many, not as a reason to size up.
